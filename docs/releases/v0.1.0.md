# Wink v0.1.0

Black out your screens with one click.

- Toggle blackout and restore with the mascot or a shortcut.
- Schedule by time and weekday, including overnight periods.
- Control blackout, restore, and status through local commands.
- English and Chinese, light and dark modes, and launch at login.

## Installation

Apple Silicon (M-series chips), macOS 13 or later. Unzip `Wink-0.1.0-macos-arm64.zip` and drag `Wink.app` into Applications.

The app is not notarized. If macOS blocks the first launch, verify the download source, then choose **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**. See [Apple’s instructions](https://support.apple.com/102445).

## Known limitations

- Blackout is not a screen lock; content may remain faintly visible on some displays.
- Connecting or disconnecting displays may briefly expose screen content.
- HDR, other color-adjustment software, sleep/wake, and long-running sessions have not been fully tested.

## 简体中文

一键黑屏。

- 点击角色或使用快捷键，调黑屏幕并恢复原来的显示状态。
- 按时间段和星期自动黑屏，支持跨天。
- 本地命令支持黑屏、恢复和状态查询。
- 支持中英文、深浅色模式和登录启动。

## 安装

Apple Silicon（M 系列芯片），macOS 13 或更高版本。解压 `Wink-0.1.0-macos-arm64.zip`，将 `Wink.app` 拖入「应用程序」。

应用未经过 Apple 公证。首次启动若被阻止，确认下载来源后，在「系统设置 → 隐私与安全性」中选择「仍要打开」。详见 [Apple 的说明](https://support.apple.com/102445) 。

## 已知限制

- 黑屏不是锁屏；部分显示器仍可能隐约显示内容。
- 拔插显示器时可能短暂亮屏，无法保证此时内容不可见。
- HDR、其他调色软件、睡眠唤醒及长时间运行的兼容性尚未全面验证。
