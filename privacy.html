Wink Privacy Policy

Wink works locally on your Mac. The app does not transmit your personal data to the developer, operate an account service, or include advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs.

Local data

Wink saves shortcut and schedule preferences on your Mac. It stores display identifiers and original Gamma values so that it can restore your displays after blackout or interruption. It may write local diagnostic messages about display changes and errors. Wink does not capture screenshots, record screen content, or record your typing.

Support

If you contact the developer, the developer receives the contact information and details you choose to send and uses them to respond to your request. Do not include passwords or sensitive screen content in support messages.

Apple services

App Store and TestFlight distribution, feedback, and diagnostics are handled by Apple under its own privacy terms and your settings. Information you choose to share through those services may be made available to the developer.
