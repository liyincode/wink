Using Wink

Press Control + Option + B to black out your displays, then press it again to restore them. The dedicated restore shortcut is Control + Option + Command + R. Right-click the menu bar icon to access settings or quit.

Wink darkens physical displays using Gamma adjustments. It does not lock your Mac or turn off the backlight. Content may remain faintly visible on some displays, and changing connected displays may briefly reveal content.

Compatibility

Apple Silicon Mac, macOS 13 or later.
